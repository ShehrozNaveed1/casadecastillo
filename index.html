Our Story

A Family Tradition
Since 1922

Almost a century of Old Mexico recipes, cooked the Texas way.

In 1922, Grandfather Antonio P. Castillo, Sr. opened the family's first restaurant, The Texas Café, in downtown Waco. Its popularity led "Mr. Tony" to open two more Castillo restaurants around town, the Aztec Café and the Italian Village, each keeping the family's Tex-Mex dishes on the menu.

As Waco grew, son Antonio P. Castillo, Jr. moved the family business to a new location in 1982 — and Casa de Castillo was born. Today the restaurant is run by grandsons Antonio III and Richard Castillo, with sister Mary Lou Castillo as Manager, still serving the same Tex-Mex with a Spanish influence the family has been known for since 1922.
